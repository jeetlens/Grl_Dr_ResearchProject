
Across three random seeds, the baseline DistilRoBERTa model achieved a mean test accuracy of 84.81 ± 0.71% and a macro-F1 score of 84.83 ± 0.71%. The DistilRoBERTa model trained with a Gradient Reversal Layer (GRL) achieved a mean test accuracy of 84.59 ± 0.84% and a macro-F1 score of 84.61 ± 0.81%. Both models demonstrated highly comparable classification performance. The baseline model maintained a small average advantage of approximately 0.22 percentage points in both accuracy and macro-F1. These findings indicate that the GRL-based adversarial training strategy did not provide a consistent performance improvement under the proxy-domain configuration used in this study.
